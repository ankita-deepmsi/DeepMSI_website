**OTTAWA — [TO CONFIRM date].** AI-Spectral Technology Corp. today introduced **Deep MSI**, a multispectral retinal imaging platform designed to capture the retina across a sequence of discrete wavelengths and assist clinicians with an AI-supported reading and reporting workflow.

Deep MSI is an **investigational device. It is not available for sale in all regions and its regulatory clearance status is [TO CONFIRM].** Nothing in this announcement constitutes a solicitation where not authorized by local law.

## What was announced

- **Sequential multispectral capture** spanning visible through near-infrared bands, producing a depth-aware photographic record of the retina. *[TO CONFIRM: spectral range, band count and acquisition time.]*
- **Spectral AI Reading**, a human-in-the-loop module that registers the spectral stack, highlights regions of interest, and assembles a structured report for clinician review.
- A workflow designed around existing clinical practice, with attention to archiving and interoperability. *[TO CONFIRM: DICOM/EMR integration specifics.]*

> "Our goal is to give eye-care professionals more measurable signal per capture, while keeping the clinician firmly in control of every decision," said a company spokesperson. *[TO CONFIRM: named spokesperson and quote approval.]*
